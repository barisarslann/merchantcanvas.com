Scope: MerchantCanvas homepage and the global marketing-site theme. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: Shopify merchants, ecommerce teams, B2B operators, and agencies deciding which focused app fits an existing workflow.

Job and action: Understand that MerchantCanvas is a multi-app Shopify product studio, distinguish promotion work from B2B quote approvals, then compare apps or request an honest fit check.

Proof: Two named products, implementation-backed definitions, verified pricing and availability language, real MultiTier screenshots deeper in the site, and practical resources.

Constraints: English-first, mobile-first, static export, no fabricated evidence, no unverified install URLs, extensible to 4–5 apps, WCAG 2.2 AA.

Direction: Swatch-book product index. Light mode uses Cobalt + Sun; dark mode uses Azure + Coral. The first viewport pairs compact direct copy with two large product routes and a factual proof strip.

Memorable moment: Theme-aware product color fields change from cobalt/sun to azure/coral while the information hierarchy remains identical.

Unresolved: Official App Store URLs, final owner-confirmed public availability, and future product categories.

Lucide Check/X vectors. Product workflow labels use high-contrast numbered
squares. The dark workflow section uses 44px step badges and separators that
end with each item's content; mobile uses 40px badges and no final divider.
