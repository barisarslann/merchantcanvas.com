# Meta acquisition pilot creative

- **Scope and mode:** Two paid-social concepts, each shipped as a 1080 × 1350
  feed asset and a 1080 × 1920 story asset; Persuade.
- **Audience:** English-speaking Shopify merchants evaluating promotion tooling.
- **Job:** Make MTD intelligible in seconds and earn a qualified visit to the
  MerchantCanvas product page.
- **Action:** “See how it works” opens the MultiTier Discounts landing page on
  MerchantCanvas; the landing page then offers the verified Shopify App Store
  install action.
- **Proof:** Approved campaign-builder screenshot, verified campaign types,
  Shopify Functions-based checkout logic, and the Offer → Target → Guardrails
  → Checkout workflow.
- **Constraints:** No fabricated adoption, ratings, revenue lift, customer
  proof, universal compatibility, or completed-install attribution.

## Chosen direction

The established Product Swatch Book world becomes a compact merchant workbench.
Creative A leads with the operating sequence and Creative B leads with promotion
mechanics. Both use Cobalt + Sun light mode because the owner prefers the light
system and the real app screenshots read most clearly on white. The dark
analytics comp remains an exploration only: its empty-state screenshot is
truthful but weaker acquisition proof.

This is a precisely specified narrow extension inside an established visual
world. Under the Impeccable new-work contract, that means concept seeding is not
required. The absent concept-seed key is therefore a formal, auditable
**narrow-extension concept-seed exception**, not an undocumented omission or a
waiver of the established Product Swatch Book direction.

## Memorable moment

The real campaign builder is crossed by a compact, readable four-step ledger or
paired with a Cobalt offer selector. The screenshot stays evidence rather than
decorative chrome.
