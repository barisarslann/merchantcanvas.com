# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Shopify merchants and ecommerce teams evaluating focused tools for promotion workflows.
- Wholesale, sales, operations, and finance teams evaluating structured B2B quote approvals.
- Shopify agencies comparing products for client stores and needing clear scope, pricing, and implementation evidence.

Visitors are usually deciding which MerchantCanvas app fits an existing operational problem. They should understand the portfolio, distinguish the apps, verify suitability, and reach the appropriate product or contact path quickly.

## Product Purpose

MerchantCanvas is a multi-product Shopify app studio and portfolio hub. It explains what each app does, who it is for, where it does not fit, how it works, and what the verified package structure is.

Success means that a first-time visitor can identify the relevant app and next action within seconds, without decoding abstract studio language or exaggerated marketing.

## Positioning

MerchantCanvas builds focused Shopify apps around clearly bounded merchant workflows. The public site uses implementation-backed claims, honest availability language, and real product evidence instead of fabricated adoption metrics, reviews, or generic SaaS promises.

## Operating Context

- Promotion teams plan tiered, quantity-break, cart-value, Buy X Get Y, and supported gift campaigns.
- B2B teams receive, review, revise, approve, share, and convert wholesale quotes into Shopify draft orders.
- Agencies need a portfolio-level overview before moving into product-specific detail.
- Product evaluation happens through landing pages, product pages, pricing, real screenshots where available, practical guides, and direct contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public portfolio currently contains MultiTier Discounts and B2B Quote Approvals and must be able to accommodate additional apps later.
- Product capabilities, limits, prices, and availability must remain aligned with their source repositories.
- MultiTier Discounts has approved real screenshots.
- B2B Quote Approvals currently has no approved public screenshots; the site must not fabricate them.
- Official public Shopify App Store or install URLs are not yet confirmed.
- The website is a statically exported, multi-route web experience prepared for Cloudflare Pages.
- Analytics and advertising integrations remain consent-gated.
- The approved hybrid color system uses Cobalt + Sun for light mode and Azure + Coral for dark mode.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the MerchantCanvas name and the existing production logo assets.
- The site must feel like one clear multi-app product portfolio, not a generic digital agency.
- The replacement visual world should use vivid, clean, high-confidence color fields inspired by the clarity of BOGOS and the cobalt/yellow energy of Newbird without copying either site.
- Light mode uses Cobalt + Sun; dark mode uses Azure + Coral. Both presentations come from one coherent semantic token system.
- Avoid oversized editorial headings, warm cream-heavy surfaces, ornamental workflow diagrams, and long abstract explanations in the first viewport.
- The first viewport must state what MerchantCanvas is, name the two current app categories, and expose a clear route into the portfolio.

## Product Principles

1. Make the portfolio understandable before making the studio memorable.
2. Route visitors by the workflow they need to improve.
3. Show verified product evidence and name limitations honestly.
4. Keep the system extensible for future apps without turning the homepage into a directory.
5. Use color and interaction to improve orientation, not to compete with the content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction requirements.
- Preserve semantic headings, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, and touch-friendly controls.
- Light and dark themes must both meet the same readability and interaction standards.
