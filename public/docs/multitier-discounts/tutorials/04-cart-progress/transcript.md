# Install Cart Progress and verify a spending offer

## V04-S01 - A spending offer, not a quantity offer

Let's install Cart Progress in the Fieldnote demo store. This widget shows how close a cart is to a spending reward.

Our example campaign already exists: spend thirty-six dollars and receive ten percent off. The widget explains that offer; it does not create the discount.

## V04-S02 - Install and save the app block

In Online Store, open the theme editor and select the Cart template. We are using an unpublished recording copy, leaving the original theme unchanged.

In a section that supports app blocks, choose Add block, Apps, and Cart Progress. Place it above the cart items so shoppers can see the next reward.

Save the theme, then preview that same draft. You can adjust the appearance later. Start by checking the offer and the amounts, rather than changing technical selectors.

## V04-S03 - Check the amount still needed

Start with an empty test cart. Add one Coral notebook, priced at twenty-four dollars, and open the cart.

The widget asks for twelve dollars more to unlock ten percent off. Twenty-four plus twelve reaches our thirty-six dollar threshold. No discount is applied yet.

## V04-S04 - Reach the reward without losing progress

Add one Sun notebook for twelve dollars. The qualifying merchandise subtotal is now thirty-six dollars, and the widget shows ten percent unlocked.

The actual discounted merchandise amount is thirty-two dollars and forty cents. The qualifying subtotal and the amount to pay are different values.

The campaign's own discount must not make the progress bar ask for another three dollars and sixty cents. Check that the unlocked state stays visible.

## V04-S05 - Verify the real checkout

Open checkout and compare both notebooks, their quantities and the ten percent discount. The merchandise subtotal remains thirty-two dollars and forty cents.

Delivery and taxes can affect the final total. We do not enter customer or payment details, and we do not place an order.

## V04-S06 - Remove, add again and finish safely

Back in the cart, remove the Sun notebook. The qualifying amount returns to twenty-four dollars, and the widget asks for twelve dollars again.

Add the Sun notebook once more. The reward unlocks again, with just one row per notebook and the same discounted amount.

Remove the test items when you finish. Check your theme on a phone as well, and keep it unpublished until the widget, cart and checkout all match the campaign.
