# Understand Analytics and Choose the Right MTD Plan

## V13-S01 - Read processed-order metrics

Open the app's Analytics page. Total Uses, Total Discount Given and Orders Affected describe recorded discount activity; Active Campaigns describes configuration. These are not interchangeable measures of conversion. Our demo has one active campaign and no processed discount orders, so No data yet is expected. We do not create a customer order just to fill a chart. A dash means missing reported data, not an estimated revenue figure.

## V13-S02 - Interpret the campaign table

Use Campaign Performance to compare the reported uses, discount amount and last-used information for each campaign. The page explains that data comes from processed orders and that historical orders before installation are not included. Adding products to a cart or opening checkout is not an order-use record. For a disputed result, compare the saved campaign, actual order lines and applied discounts. Do not treat this table as session replay, acquisition attribution or proof of incremental sales.

## V13-S03 - Choose features rather than the largest plan

In Plans and Billing, start with the offer you need. Free covers core percentage offers within its campaign allowance. Starter adds capabilities such as codes and Buy X Get Y; Pro adds more advanced gifts and targeting. Read the actual current comparison and limits here. This is a development store with test access, not evidence that a real merchant has subscribed or been charged. No plan purchase is made in this recording.

## V13-S04 - Separate app Plus from Shopify Plus

MTD Plus is the app subscription; Shopify Plus is the store's platform plan. Supported checkout mystery needs the relevant app entitlement, an eligible Shopify Plus store and the displayed configuration requirements. Buying the app plan cannot turn a non-Plus Shopify store into Plus. Review Shopify's actual confirmation, price and trial terms before approving any charge. Finish by matching the chosen features to one tested offer; upgrade decisions should not replace correct campaign setup and checkout checks.
