# Configure Buy X Get Y on Shopify

## V07-S01 - Choose the direct gift format

Let's configure a Buy X Get Y offer in the Fieldnote demo. Buy the Coral notebook and receive the Sun notebook free. From Create New Campaign, choose Buy X Get Y, give the offer a recognizable name and keep automatic execution. This format fixes the gift discount at one hundred percent. It is different from a percentage quantity break or a configurable Mystery Gift pool.

## V07-S02 - Select buy and reward separately

In Conditions, select Coral as the qualifying purchase. Confirm the Shopify picker, then wait for the returned product card before moving on. A whole-product selection includes its variants. In Rewards, select Sun as the gift target. Selecting the buy item never selects the gift for you. Check the displayed free-gift quantity and the exact target; an unrelated product outside this selection is not a substitute.

## V07-S03 - Save an inactive draft

Review the buy scope, free reward, schedule and combination settings. Save as inactive draft, then reopen Conditions and Rewards to check the stored selections. This recording verifies the saved setup; it does not activate this offer or claim a new checkout result. Eligible automatic insertion also needs Gift Auto-Add enabled in the exact theme being tested. A visible Quantity Tiers block alone does not enable gifts.

## V07-S04 - Check the intended purchase path

Before your own launch, activate only for a controlled test. Check a cart without Coral, then add Coral and confirm the intended Sun quantity and a zero gift price before checkout. Remove the gift manually and confirm it stays removed for the unchanged purchase. Change the paid quantity, remove the purchase and repeat the cycle. Compare variants, quantities and totals in real checkout. Use the separate Gift Auto-Add tutorial for that hands-on cart workflow.
