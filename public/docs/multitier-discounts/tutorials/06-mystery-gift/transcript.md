# Create and verify a Mystery Gift on Shopify Plus

## V06-S01 - Mystery Gift and its requirements

Let's build a Mystery Gift in the Fieldnote demo store. Spend fifty dollars and receive one free gift, displayed as Mystery Notebook.

Cart and checkout presentation requires a Shopify Plus store, the MTD Plus plan, granted checkout permissions and a supported gift campaign. Storefront cart only is a separate option.

We will use an unpublished theme copy and a temporary campaign, leaving the existing demo setup unchanged.

## V06-S02 - Create the offer and exact variant pool

Create a new Gift offer, give it a descriptive name and keep it automatic. In Conditions, choose Cart value.

In Rewards, set the minimum to fifty U.S. dollars and the gift discount to one hundred percent.

Select the exact Cobalt and Sun variants for the gift pool. Checkout mystery needs explicit variants, not just a whole-product selection. Only one eligible gift will be delivered.

Our temporary Gift Notebook has three editions. Tick Cobalt and Sun individually, and leave Coral unselected. The product's parent checkbox selects the whole product, not this exact variant pool.

## V06-S03 - Choose delivery and mystery presentation

Open Gift delivery and choose Random one from selected gifts. Keep a fixed quantity of one. Automatic addition is enabled for this mode.

In Advanced gift settings, turn on Keep the selected gift a mystery. Enter Mystery Notebook and choose Storefront cart and checkout.

For this walkthrough, keep the original product image. The product title is neutral, but the image and variant label can still identify the selection. A neutral image is a separate presentation choice.

If checkout is locked, check the displayed plan, permissions and campaign requirements. Do not remove safeguards or change unrelated settings to force it on.

## V06-S04 - Review and save an inactive draft

Review the fifty-dollar threshold, one hundred percent reward, exact variant pool and fixed gift quantity. Keep extra targeting, limits and discount combinations unchanged for this isolated example.

Save the campaign as an inactive draft. Reopen it and confirm the title, checkout surface and pool were saved before activating it.

Different currencies, countries and discount codes need their own tests. This U.S. dollar demonstration does not certify every checkout configuration.

## V06-S05 - Enable and test the qualifying cart

Make sure Gift Auto-Add is enabled and saved in the theme you are previewing. This is an app embed, separate from the discount widgets. Then activate only this test campaign.

Start with an empty cart. Two Coral notebooks cost forty-eight dollars, so no mystery gift should be added.

Increase Coral to three. The paid purchase is now seventy-two dollars. One Mystery Notebook should appear free before you press checkout. Wait for the actual gift result before checking its price.

## V06-S06 - Verify the actual Plus checkout

Open the real checkout. Confirm three Coral notebooks and exactly one free Mystery Notebook. The merchandise amount remains seventy-two dollars.

Mystery presentation changes the shopper-facing title, not the original product variant or SKU used for fulfillment. It does not create a replacement product.

Check quantities and the free price, not only the title. Stop if the gift is charged or a warning blocks checkout. We enter no customer or payment details and place no order.

## V06-S07 - Verify removal and clean up safely

Return to the cart and manually remove Mystery Notebook. It should stay removed for the unchanged paid purchase, including after a refresh.

Change the paid quantity from three Coral notebooks to four. This new qualifying purchase can receive one gift again, without duplicate gifts.

Remove the paid purchase and confirm the gift is cleaned up. Repeat the qualifying add and removal check, then finish with an empty cart.

After filming, we remove only our temporary campaign, theme and checkout test resources. Existing campaigns remain unchanged. Keep your own launch inactive until your intended markets and codes pass their checks.
