# Install Quantity Tiers and verify the offer

## V03-S01 - Choose the right widget

Let's install Quantity Tiers with MultiTier Discounts, using the Fieldnote demo store.

Quantity Tiers explains a product's quantity discount. Cart Progress is a different widget for spending offers. This walkthrough focuses on Quantity Tiers, from installation to the real checkout.

## V03-S02 - Choose the theme and add Quantity Tiers

In Online Store, open Themes and choose the theme you want to test. We are using an unpublished recording copy, not the live theme.

In the theme editor, open the Cobalt notebook's product template. Find a section that supports app blocks, then choose Add block, Apps, and Quantity Tiers.

Place the block beside the purchase controls. Make sure you are editing the template used by this product, rather than a different product template.

The campaign already exists: two Cobalt notebooks receive ten percent off, and three or more receive fifteen percent off. The widget displays the offer; it does not create the discount.

## V03-S03 - Customize, save and preview

Open the Quantity Tiers settings. Give it a short title and a subtitle that matches the campaign. Keep Show quantity selector enabled for this example.

Save the draft theme, then preview that same theme and product. Start with the default integration settings; you do not need technical selectors for this standard setup.

Choose two in the widget and add them to the cart. Confirm two notebooks and thirty-two dollars and forty cents. Test the widget's own button instead of assuming it follows the theme's separate quantity field.

Empty the test cart before the next example.

## V03-S04 - Check the next quantity tier

Now check the next tier on the same Cobalt product. Select three in the widget. The campaign gives fifteen percent off each qualifying notebook, not ten plus fifteen percent.

The widget estimates forty-five dollars and ninety cents for three notebooks. Add them with its own button and compare that estimate with the real cart.

Confirm three notebooks, fifteen percent off, and forty-five dollars and ninety cents. The original price is fifty-four dollars; the saving is eight dollars and ten cents.

## V03-S05 - Verify the message and checkout

Open checkout and compare the same product, quantity and discount. For three Cobalt notebooks, the discounted merchandise amount remains forty-five dollars and ninety cents.

Shipping and taxes may be separate. Check the merchandise calculation rather than expecting every checkout total to ignore delivery costs.

We will not enter contact or payment details, and we will not place an order. Return to the cart when the amounts match.

## V03-S06 - Check removal and mobile

Return to the cart and remove the notebooks. The cart should become empty, with no duplicate rows or stale discount left behind.

Use the theme editor's mobile preview to inspect text and spacing. Previewing the layout is useful, but also test the actual product and cart on a phone before launch.

Gift Auto-Add is a separate app embed for gift campaigns. Quantity Tiers does not require you to enable it, and this tutorial does not change its settings.

## V03-S07 - Finish safely

Our test cart is empty. The disposable recording theme can now be removed; the original demo campaign and theme stay unchanged.

If a widget is missing, check the theme, template, block placement and campaign eligibility first. If the actual discount is wrong, check the campaign rather than changing the widget's colors or selectors.

Keep your own theme as a draft until the product page, cart and checkout all match the offer. The written widget guide includes these checks for your own store.
