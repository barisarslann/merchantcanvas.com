# Distribution, fundraising, and exit channel scan — 2026-08-09

## Decision

Do not treat every platform as the same funnel. MerchantCanvas currently needs
customer acquisition and proof before it needs fundraising or an exit market.

1. **Prepare now:** Shoffi affiliate pilot, value-first Shopify Community
   participation, and a free G2 profile after category-fit confirmation.
2. **Prepare later:** a free Product Hunt launch when the demo/support path is
   launch-ready, and OpenVC only after a coherent traction story and deck exist.
3. **Do not use now:** Reditus, PartnerStack, Acquire.com, Flippa, Microns, or a
   strategic acquirer process. They are paid, exit-oriented, or require more
   traction than MTD currently has.

## Channel map

| Channel | Job | Current public economics | Fit now | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shoffi | Shopify-app affiliate recruitment and attribution | Free until $100/month in affiliate-generated revenue; 0% platform commission on affiliate payouts | High | Prepare EXP-003; security and owner gates first |
| Shopify Community | Direct problem discovery and trust through helpful answers | Free; community rules prohibit spam-like promotion | High if value-first | Draft exact answers; owner approves every post |
| G2 | Software profile, verified reviews, and search/AI discovery | Basic profile and review collection are free | Medium | Confirm category fit, then prepare a truthful profile |
| Product Hunt | Product-launch awareness and feedback | Regular new-product launch is free; promoted placement is paid | Medium-low merchant intent | Prepare only after launch assets and support are ready |
| OpenVC | Investor discovery, deck sharing/analytics, and fundraising CRM | Core platform is free; Premium is optional | Low today, useful later | Build readiness first; no investor outreach yet |
| Microns | Sale of a small profitable startup | No listing fee; 10%/8%/6% success fee by sale size; requires paying customers, traction, profitability, and at least five months of history | Too early | Revisit only if an exit becomes the owner's objective |
| Acquire.com | Startup/company sale marketplace | Under $250k ask: $25/month plus 8% closing fee | Too early and not free | Defer |
| Flippa | Digital asset/company sale marketplace | Seller listing starts at $29 plus a success fee | Too early and not free | Defer |
| Shop Circle | Strategic buyer/operator of Shopify apps | Relationship-led acquisition, not a self-serve free listing | Too early | Keep as a future strategic-acquirer reference |
| Reditus | General B2B SaaS affiliate platform | No free plan; $99/month entry and marketplace listing from $399/month annual plan | Poor budget fit | Skip while Shoffi remains unvalidated |
| PartnerStack | Large B2B partner network | Vendor plans start at $1,000/month billed annually | Poor budget fit | Skip |

## Recommended zero-cost sequence

1. Complete Shoffi's token, permission, attribution, and owner-approval gates.
2. Prepare a G2 profile using only current product facts, official pricing, and
   voluntary reviews from real users who have received value.
3. Build a small library of Shopify Community answers from observed merchant
   questions. No drive-by links, aliases, or undisclosed promotion.
4. Use Product Hunt only as a scheduled launch moment with a clear demo,
   founder comment, screenshots, and a support response plan.
5. Revisit OpenVC after MTD has a defensible customer, retention, review, and
   revenue narrative. Fundraising is not a substitute for traction.
