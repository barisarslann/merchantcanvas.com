# Durable Learnings

Only add a learning when it is supported by an authoritative source, repeated
observation, or completed experiment. Observations that have not reached that
threshold belong in reports or experiment notes.

## Channel economics

- **Shopify App Store Ads are excluded for the current stage.** The owner has
  already tested the channel and observed click economics that are incompatible
  with the available budget. Reconsider only after an explicit owner decision,
  not because a generic benchmark recommends the channel.

## Measurement

- An App Store handoff is not a completed installation. MerchantCanvas uses
  `install_intent` for the former and Shopify Partner Dashboard for the latter.
- AI referral attribution is incomplete by nature. Only identified referrers
  may be reported as verified AI-referred sessions; direct/unknown traffic must
  remain unknown.
- Shopify Partner Dashboard's raw merchant count is not automatically a paying
  customer count. Internal/test stores must be excluded before it is used as a
  growth KPI.

## Current discovery state

- The technical delivery foundation was healthy on 2026-08-02, but public
  search discovery still appeared immature. This suggests the immediate gap is
  distribution/indexation evidence rather than another broad technical
  redesign. Search Console must confirm or revise this interpretation.
