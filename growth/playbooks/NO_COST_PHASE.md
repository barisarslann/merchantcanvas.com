# Zero-Cost Phase Playbook

## Objective

Produce trustworthy acquisition evidence and launch-ready assets without paid
media, external posting, direct outreach, or review solicitation.

## Week 1 — Observe and prepare

1. Verify read-only access to Shopify Partner Dashboard, Search Console, GA4,
   and Bing. Record unavailable sources explicitly.
2. Record completed installs, uninstalls, subscriptions, and public review count
   as separate fields where available.
3. Verify the live consent and `install_intent` implementation with current
   production IDs if they exist; do not add IDs without approval.
4. Review active ads in Meta Ad Library for the relevant Shopify discount-app
   category. Record hooks, formats, proof styles, and calls to action, not copied
   artwork or unsupported competitor claims.
5. Collect real problem language from public questions. Participate nowhere and
   contact nobody during observation mode.

## Week 2 — Convert evidence into one test

1. Repeat the fixed AI/search query sample on or after 2026-08-12.
2. Score the existing MTD content backlog using customer impact, product fit,
   observed search demand, and production effort.
3. Prepare one answer-first content brief or one Meta creative brief.
4. Define its hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, and stop condition before
   asking for publication or spend approval.
5. Produce the first weekly readiness report.

## Free distribution candidates

- Improve and index existing high-intent MTD guides.
- Publish one implementation-backed MTD article after owner review.
- Prepare a bounded Shoffi affiliate pilot for MultiTier Discounts. Shoffi is
  an affiliate acquisition channel, not an investor or app-sale marketplace.
  The proposed starting terms are 30% of eligible net recurring revenue for
  12 months, $0 CPI, manual affiliate approval, and a $50 minimum withdrawal.
  Nothing is published until the owner approves the final terms and security
  gates.
- Claim a free G2 profile only after category fit is confirmed. Use verified
  product facts and voluntary customer reviews; never manufacture, gate, or
  incentivize reviews.
- Prepare one value-first Shopify Community answer format. Link to MTD only
  when it directly solves the merchant's stated problem and the owner approves
  the exact reply.
- Prepare a Product Hunt draft after the product demo and support path are
  strong enough for launch-day questions. Treat it as awareness, not as a
  merchant-intent or installation channel.
- Prepare authentic, disclosed answers to existing community questions; posting
  remains approval-required and must follow each community's rules.
- Build a Shopify-agency evaluation checklist from verified product evidence.
- Prepare onboarding and honest-review request language for use only after a
  merchant has received value.

## Shoffi affiliate pilot gate

The proposed Shoffi experiment remains preparation-only until all of the
following are true:

1. The owner rotates the Shopify Partner API token referenced by Shoffi's
   dashboard security notice and confirms the old token is revoked.
2. The owner explicitly approves Shoffi's requested `View financials` and
   `Manage apps` permissions and the exact affiliate terms.
3. MultiTier Discounts attribution is verified in a separate task rooted in
   the MTD repository if app-side work is required. This repository must not
   modify the MTD application.
4. A final preview confirms the official App Store destination, truthful app
   claims, manual affiliate approval, $0 CPI, and no review incentive.
5. The owner authorizes the external Shoffi listing. Until then, the account
   remains unintegrated and no affiliate is invited or approved.

After activation, use a 30-day observation window. The primary success signal
is at least one non-test completed Shopify install attributable to an approved,
relevant affiliate. Shoffi clicks and referrals remain diagnostics; they are
not counted as installs or customers.

## Exit gate

The no-cost phase is complete only when:

- the primary measurement sources are understood;
- legal/consent blockers are explicit;
- one channel hypothesis has a bounded test design;
- at least three compliant creative/message variants exist;
- the owner explicitly authorizes a platform and hard budget cap.
