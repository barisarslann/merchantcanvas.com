# Meta restriction review draft

**Status:** Owner review required; do not submit automatically  
**Observed:** 2026-08-03  
**Restriction:** Business portfolio cannot create/run ads or use/share audiences

## What Meta reported

Meta says the portfolio appeared to be created or used with automation that
does not follow its Account Integrity rules. The visible examples concerned
bulk machine activity, activity that mimics a person, and automation that
replaces authentic human activity.

## Draft review statement

MerchantCanvas is a legitimate software business publishing tools for Shopify
merchants. The business portfolio was created to support the published
MultiTier Discounts app and the MerchantCanvas website.

We do not intend to use prohibited automation, bulk account creation, simulated
human activity, or any method that circumvents Meta's enforcement. Advertising
activity will remain under the business owner's direct oversight, use standard
Meta tools, follow explicit spending caps, and make only truthful, verifiable
product claims. Tracking will load only after the appropriate visitor consent.

Please manually review the MerchantCanvas portfolio and let us know whether any
additional identity, business, domain, or product evidence is required.

## Evidence to have ready

- Live business domain: `https://merchantcanvas.com`
- Published product listing:
  `https://apps.shopify.com/multitier-discounts`
- Domain-controlled support email access
- Owner identity and business-email confirmation
- Screenshot of Meta's restriction notice and the review case identifier, if
  Meta provides one
- A plain-language description of intended ads, markets, budget cap, and manual
  owner oversight

## Submission guardrails

- The owner must verify every sentence before submission.
- Do not claim that no automation ever touched the account unless the owner can
  personally verify that statement.
- Do not upload identity or business documents into this repository.
- Submit through Meta's official `Request review` flow only.
- Record only the case status and non-sensitive case identifier afterward.
