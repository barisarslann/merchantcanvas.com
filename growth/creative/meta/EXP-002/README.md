# EXP-002 Meta creative package

These files are the selected 4:5 acquisition creatives for the first paused
MultiTier Discounts Meta pilot.

- `mtd-workflow-control-1080x1350.png` and its 1080 × 1920 placement
  variant test the operating-sequence message.
- `mtd-promotion-mechanics-1080x1350.png` and its 1080 × 1920 placement
  variant test the campaign-shape message.
- `manifest.json` owns the exact ad copy, UTM destinations, campaign shape,
  spend ceiling, and reporting layers.

Both creative concepts use the approved MerchantCanvas light system and the real
`multitier-campaign-rules.jpg` product screenshot. They were composed as
repo-native HTML/CSS and rendered in 4:5 and 9:16 formats. No generated or
fabricated product interface, adoption statistic, review, customer proof, or
performance result appears in either creative.

The package is not authorization to publish or spend. The campaign, ad set, and
ads must remain paused until the owner approves the final Meta preview.
