## Visible-content changes

- Homepage and `/apps` now lead with MTD as the only live product.
- Every MTD conversion CTA uses “Install from Shopify”; contact remains a
  secondary fit-check path on the product page.
- B2B cards and product content say “Coming soon,” retain indexability, and
  expose only a launch-update CTA.
- MTD shows Free, Starter, and Pro in the existing three-card layout and the
  Shopify Plus-only plan in a separate full-width conditional band.
- MTD FAQ and pricing copy explain why the public listing can show three
  standard plans while verified Shopify Plus stores can see the conditional
  Plus plan.
- The quantity-break guide now ends with a direct official App Store CTA.

## Claim constraints

- Use only capabilities and prices verified in the product repository and
  official App Store listing.
- Do not claim revenue/AOV lift, review volume, merchant count, market
  leadership, or universal compatibility.
- Describe an App Store click as install intent, not installation.
- Keep B2B out of ad campaigns until it has a verified public release path.
- Keep Privacy and Terms visibly marked as drafts until the owner approves the
  final legal text.
